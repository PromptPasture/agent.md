# Lifestyle skills

Skills for non-software domains. Fully conversational, one question at a time.

## Skills

### `lawyer`

A conversational legal advisor for regular people without legal education. Acts as a knowledgeable friend — plain language, no jargon, no legal opinion disclaimers buried in fine print.

**Modes (all in one skill):** contract review · legal drafting · legal Q&A · compliance review.

**Key behaviors:**

- Opens every session with a disclaimer: "This is not legal advice…"
- Asks for jurisdiction (country/region) early in every session
- Responds in the user's language
- Flags risks at two tiers: "worth knowing" and "serious red flag — consult a lawyer"
- Supports multi-document sessions

**Written output:** Legal Memo on request. Sections: Summary · Jurisdiction · Key Findings · Recommendations · Open Questions · Disclaimer. Default path: `docs/YYYY-MM-DD-[topic]/LEGAL-MEMO.md`.

### `landscape-design`

Transforms outdoor spaces into functional, beautiful landscapes using expert plant knowledge, architectural styling, and adaptive conversation. Triggers on "landscape design", "yard design", "garden planning", "plant selection", "backyard makeover", or "landscaping ideas".
